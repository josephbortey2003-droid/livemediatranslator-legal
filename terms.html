1. The service

The app shows translated subtitles for audio playing on your phone or picked up by its microphone. It offers:

  • Offline mode – free, using language models downloaded to your phone. Which languages are available depends on your phone.
  • Online mode – cloud translation provided through Soniox, which uses cloud minutes from your balance and needs an internet connection.

2. Cloud minutes

  • Cloud minutes are measured by our server from the moment an online session starts until it stops, in seconds, with a minimum of 15 seconds per session.
  • When a session starts, up to 10 minutes are reserved from your balance; whatever is not used is returned when the session ends. Sessions reconnect automatically about every 10 minutes, and changing settings such as the target language starts a new session.
  • If your balance runs out, the app switches to Offline mode when a suitable offline language is installed, or stops and tells you.
  • Cloud minutes have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and are tied to this installation’s guest account. Deleting your cloud data, or uninstalling the app while account sign-in is not yet available, means any remaining minutes are lost.
  • Buying minutes or subscriptions is not available yet. When it is, prices will be shown in your local currency by the store you buy from, and these terms will be updated with the purchase and refund rules before purchases go live.
